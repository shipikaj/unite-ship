Google Ads Custom Report Fields

192 metrics · 557 dimensions

Complete list of Google Ads fields that can be used in a Unite custom report. Use a custom report when the prebuilt reports do not include the fields you need.

Dimensions and metrics​

There are two types of fields: dimensions and metrics.

  • Dimensions describe or split your data, such as campaign name, ad group, date, or country. Segments (segments.*) are dimensions that break metrics down into more rows. For example, adding segments.date returns one row per day.
  • Metrics (metrics.*) are numeric values, such as clicks, impressions, cost, and conversions.

Monetary fields ending in _micros are expressed in micros, one millionth of the account currency unit. Divide by 1,000,000 to get the amount in the account currency.

Combining dimensions and metrics​

Not every metric can be combined with every segment or resource field. To check whether a combination is supported, use Google's field compatibility rules.
