Connect Meta Ads

This guide explains how to connect your Meta Ads account as a data source in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your Meta Ads data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

Before you begin​

Before connecting, make sure you have:​

  • A Facebook account
  • A Meta Ads account (at least one active Ad Account)
  • Your role on the Ad Account must be Advertiser, Editor, or Admin

If your Ad Account is under a Business Manager, your Facebook account must be added as a member of that Business Manager.

Connect your account​

Step 1​

AUTHENTICATE​
  1. Click "Add Your Credentials" button
  2. You'll be taken to Facebook's login page — log in if not already
  3. Facebook will show a screen like this: "Review Unite's access request" Receive your email address Access your Facebook ads and related stats
  4. Click Continue — you'll be redirected back to Unite automatically

Next step​

After connecting your Meta Ads account, create a pipeline to begin syncing data.
