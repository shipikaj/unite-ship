# Welcome to Unite

Unite is a no-code ETL platform that helps you connect your data sources, sync them to your destinations, and monitor everything in one place.

Whether you are working with marketing data, product analytics, or CRM tools, Unite makes your data pipeline simple and reliable.

---

## What you can do with Unite

- Connect tools like Google Ads, Shopify, Meta Ads, and more  
- Send data to destinations like BigQuery, Snowflake, and Google Sheets  
- Automate data syncing with scheduled pipelines  
- Get alerts when something breaks  
- Collaborate with your team  

---

## How Unite works

Unite follows a simple three-step flow:

1. Connect source  
   Bring in data from platforms like ads, analytics, or CRM tools  

2. Connect destination  
   Choose where your data should go  

3. Create pipeline  
   Define how and when your data should sync  

Unite handles the extraction, transformation, and loading automatically.

## Your workspace

Once inside Unite, you will see:

- Dashboard for an overview of sync status and activity  
- Data sources for all connected platforms  
- Destinations for where your data is stored  
- Pipelines for active data flows  
- Alerts for issues and notifications
