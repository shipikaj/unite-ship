# Connect Google Analytics 4

This guide explains how to connect your Google Analytics 4 account as a data source in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your Google Analytics 4 data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

---

## Before you begin

#### To connect Google Analytics 4 to Unite, you need following things 

- A Google user account with permissions to access Google Analytics accounts with Google Analytics 4 properties you'd like to sync.

---

## Connect your account

### Step 1

##### 1. Add Credential

Click "Add your credential" CTA
![1.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/1_a4e86f7a7d.png)

---

### Step 2

##### 2. Sign in with Google
A consent screen will appear. Select the Google account you want to connect.
![2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/2_884cde50cb.png)


Select the checkbox to give permission to Unite read your data and then click continue.
![3.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/3_83ce82db1d.png)

---


## Next step

After connecting your Google Analytics 4 account, create a pipeline to begin syncing data.
