# Connect HubSpot

This guide explains how to connect your HubSpot account as a data source in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your HubSpot data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

---

## Before you begin

#### To connect HubSpot to Unite
You need a Hubspot with an account.

---

## Connect your account

### Step 1

##### 1. Add Credential
Click "Add Your Credentials" button.
![1.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/1_6a6ab01138.png)

---

### Step 2

##### 2.Select Account
Select one of the account which you want to connect to Unite.
![2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/2_4596979633.png)

---


## Next step

After connecting your HubSpot account, create a pipeline to begin syncing data.
