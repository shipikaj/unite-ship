# Connect Google Ads

This guide explains how to connect your Google Ads account as a data source in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your Google Ads data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

---

## Before you begin

#### To connect with Google Ads to unite
- You need a Google Ads account.

---

## Connect your account

### Step 1

##### 1. Add Credential

Click "Add your credential" button
![1.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/1_79da6d6930.png)

---

### Step 2

##### 2. Sign in with Google
A consent screen will appear. Select the Google account you want to connect.
![2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/2_fc360c782b.png)


Review the permission that Unite need and then click Allow.
![3.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/3_76cc80626c.png)

---


## Next step

After connecting your Google Ads account, create a pipeline to begin syncing data.
