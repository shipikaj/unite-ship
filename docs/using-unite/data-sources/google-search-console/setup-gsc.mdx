# Connect Google Search Console

This guide explains how to connect your Google Search Console account as a data source in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your Google Search Console data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

---

## Before you begin

#### To connect Google ADs to Unite, you need:

- A Google Search Console account set up with you as a verified owner of the sites.
- Access to your Google Search Console sites with a Full User or Owner role.

---

## Connect your account

### Step 1

##### 1. Add Credential

Click "Add your credential" button
![1.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/1_84f9371575.png)

---

### Step 2

##### 2. Sign in with Google
A consent screen will appear. Select the Google account you want to connect.
![2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/2_fc360c782b.png)


Review the permission that Unite will need for google search console and then click Allow.
![2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/2_d2c50a2b4e.png)

---


## Next step

After connecting your Google Search Console account, create a pipeline to begin syncing data.
