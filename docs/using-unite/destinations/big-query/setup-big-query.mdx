# Connect BigQuery

This guide explains how to connect your BigQuery account as a data destination in Unite.

Once connected, you can use your BigQuery data to build pipelines and sync data to your destination.

---

## Before you begin

#### To connect BigQuery to Unite, you need the following:
- A BigQuery account or a Google Apps account
- A Unite user account with permissions to create or manage destinations.

---

## Connect your account

### Step 1

##### 1. FIND PROJECT ID
- You need to grant Unite access to your BigQuery cluster so we can create and manage tables for your data, and periodically load data into those tables.
- Go to your Google Cloud Console's projects list.
- Find your project on the list and make a note of the Project ID in the ID column. You will need it to configure Unite.

---

### Step 2

#####  2. FIND SERVICE ACCOUNT

- Log in to your Unite account.
- Go to the Destinations page, and then click Add destination.
- In the pop-up window, enter a Destination name of your choice.
- Click Add.
- Select BigQuery as the destination type.
- Enter the Project ID you found in Step 1.
- Make a note of the Unite service account. You will need to grant it permissions in BigQuery
![0.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/0_a0d6ec27d6.png)

---

### Step 3

##### 3. CONFIGURE SERVICE ACCOUNT

- Go back to the IAM & admin tab, and go to the project principals list.
- Select + GRANT ACCESS.
- In the New Principals field, enter the Unite service account you found in Step 3
- The service account is the entire email address.
- Click Select a role > BigQuery > "BigQuery User" and "BigQuery Data Editor"

![bq_guide_2.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/bq_guide_2_bed0a20df1.png)

![bq_guide_3.png](https://d1y8p27odt6lnh.cloudfront.net/strapi-upload/bq_guide_3_cb8a0d69ea.png)

---

### Step 4

##### 4. COMPLETE SETUP 

- Log in to your Unite account.
- Go to the Destinations page and click Add destination.
- Enter a Destination name of your choice and then click Add.
- Select BigQuery as the destination type.
- In the destination setup form, enter the Project ID you found in Step 2.
- Set the Service Account.
- Enter the Data Location.
- Choose your Time zone.
- Click Save & Test.

Unite tests and validates the BigQuery connection. On successful completion of the setup tests, you can sync your data to the BigQuery destination using Unite.
